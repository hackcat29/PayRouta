# Activate payments

You give PayRouta only what the ways you get paid need. Taking crypto needs a name and a country. Cards, bank transfers and mobile money need more, and how much depends on whether you're an individual or a registered business and on how much you sell.

Open **Settings → Activate payments**.

## Your details

Choose your seller type, enter your name or your business's name, your country and how much you expect to sell, then accept PayRouta's merchant terms.

| Seller type | For |
| --- | --- |
| Individual | Selling without a registered business |
| Sole proprietor | A registered business name |
| Registered company, partnership or non-profit | A registered business |

## Crypto and stablecoins

Crypto paid into your own [NEAR Intents account](near-intents.md) or receive addresses, kept as crypto, needs nothing more. PayRouta runs an automatic sanctions check on your name; once it's clear, choose **Go live with crypto**. There are no documents and no review.

[Cashing out to your bank](cash-out.md) is a payout in local currency, so it needs your identity: the same identity details and ID as cards and bank payments.

## Verified tick

Customers see a tick beside your business name at checkout, on payment links and on invoices once PayRouta has verified who you are: your government ID (and, for a registered business, its registration certificate) accepted by our review team, and a clear sanctions check. Cards, bank transfers, mobile money and cashing out to a bank need these documents anyway. If you only take and keep crypto, you don't have to send them; to get the tick, upload them under **Verified tick**. Merchants without the tick can take payments as usual, and checkout shows nothing in its place.

## Your logo and contact links

Under **Settings → Business**, upload your logo: a PNG, JPEG or WebP up to 256 KB. Customers see it in place of your initial on checkout, payment links and invoices. Your business name links back to your store, and your website and support email from **Your business** appear as links at the bottom of checkout.

## Cards, bank transfers and mobile money

What's needed grows with your business. Each item shows as **Needed**, **In review**, **Done**, **To grow** or **Later**.

| | Individual | Registered business | Higher volume |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly sales | Up to $10,000 | Up to $50,000 | Over $50,000 |
| What you sell and a support email | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Date of birth, phone, address and ID number | Yours | Your director's | Your director's |
| Photo of your ID | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Payout bank account | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Registration number and certificate, business address |  | ✓ | ✓ |
| Owners with 25% or more, with their details |  |  | ✓ |
| Proof of address |  |  | ✓ |

A registered business that expects more than $50,000 a month gives the higher-volume items before review. When you outgrow your tier, the next tier's items show as **To grow**; payments carry on while you add them. An individual selling more than $10,000 a month is asked to register the business.

Documents can be PDFs, PNGs or JPEGs under 5 MB. They're stored privately and scanned, and PayRouta reviews each one. You can submit for review while a document is still in review.

Once everything marked **Needed** is in, choose **Submit for review**. PayRouta reviews your account and opens it with its processing partners; you see **Submitted**, **In review** and **Active**.

Identity and bank details are stored encrypted and never shown again in full.
