# Binance Pay

**Category**: [Crypto Apps](providers/crypto-apps/README.md)  
**Provider**: Binance  
**Method ID**: `crypto-app`  
**Integration Mode**: `STANDARD` / `CONFIGURABLE`

Binance Pay is a contactless, borderless, zero-gas cryptocurrency payment technology that allows users to send and receive crypto payments worldwide.

---

## Technical Flow

- **Direct In-App Push**: Binance users scan a dynamic QR code or approve an app notification.
- **Zero Gas Fees**: Payments clear immediately off-chain across Binance's internal ledger.
- **Multi-Currency Auto-Conversion**: Shoppers can spend supported crypto assets while the merchant receives exact USDC or USD into their Binance Merchant account.
- **FIU / AML Compliance**: Transactions are pre-screened in accordance with jurisdictional requirements.
