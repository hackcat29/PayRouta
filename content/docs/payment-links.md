# Payment links

No website or online store? Create a payment link in your dashboard and share it anywhere: WhatsApp, Instagram, SMS, email, an invoice or a QR code. Each customer who opens it pays on PayRouta checkout with any method you choose, including [pay with credit](credit.md).

## Create a link

1. In your dashboard, open **Payment links**.
2. Enter what it's for, the amount and currency, and the country most of your customers are in.
3. Choose the ways to pay: card, digital wallets, bank transfer, mobile money, USSD, pay with credit, and stablecoins for USD links.
4. Copy the link, or share it straight to WhatsApp.

## What customers see

The link opens a page with your business name, what they're paying for and the amount. When they press **Pay**, PayRouta opens their own checkout, so one link takes any number of payments. Opening the page creates nothing, so link previews in chat apps are safe.

Credit plans are shown for the customer's own country when PayRouta can tell where they are, otherwise for the country you chose.

## After payment

Customers land on a PayRouta thank-you page. Each payment appears under **Transactions** with the link's name as its reference, and you get the usual [webhooks](webhooks.md) if you've set them up.

## Turning a link off

Turn a link off from the same page when you stop selling the item. Its page then shows nothing to pay, and you can turn it back on later.
