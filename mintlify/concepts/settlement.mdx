Your providers settle to you directly, into the destination you configured in your own account with each provider. PayRouta never receives, holds or forwards your funds. What PayRouta does is **track and reconcile** settlement across all your providers in one place.

## Confirmation versus settlement

These are separate facts:

| Fact | Meaning | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmation | The provider accepted the customer's funds | `payment.confirmed` |
| Settlement | The provider reported paying your share to you | `settlement.completed` |

Fulfil orders on confirmation. Settlement timing follows each provider's own schedule, which may be daily or weekly, and differs between methods.

## Reconciliation

PayRouta matches:

- each confirmed payment
- the provider's settlement report
- the fee the provider actually deducted
- the allocation of each settlement to individual payments

Matching is by exact reference, never by amount or date alone. These differences become **exceptions**, which PayRouta raises instead of adjusting silently:

- missing payments
- conflicting outcomes
- amount or currency differences
- fee mismatches
- partial or unallocated settlements
- funds that arrive after an intent expired
- outcomes still unknown after five minutes

Exceptions appear in the dashboard under **Payment issues** and are sent as `settlement.exception` events.

## Settlement API

`GET /settlements` lists settlements that include your payments. Amounts are integer strings in minor units; `gross`, `fee` and `net` cover only your allocations. `reconciliation` reports whether the provider's figures matched.

## Records

PayRouta keeps an operational journal of what each provider reported for your payments. It is a record of your providers' activity, **not** a balance held by PayRouta.

## Split-Settlement & Licensed Partner Architecture

PayRouta operates strictly as a non-custodial software orchestration and ledger layer while physical fund holding, interbank clearing, card acquiring, and stablecoin liquidity run through licensed sponsor banks and regulated payment partners (`lib/treasury/platforms/`):

| Partner Module (`id`) | Regulated Role | Settlement Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `monnify-split` | CBN-Licensed NIBSS Dedicated Virtual Accounts & Split Settlement | Provisions 10-digit NGN virtual accounts (`incomeSplitConfig`) and splits merchant net vs. PayRouta fee at the NIBSS clearing layer. |
| `paystack-split` | Licensed Subaccount Split Acquiring (`NG`, `GH`, `KE`, `ZA`) | Routes card, bank transfer, USSD, and mobile money into merchant subaccounts with atomic `transaction_charge` fee deduction. |
| `flutterwave-split` | Pan-African Subaccount Split Acquiring | Splits multi-country card and mobile money (`M-Pesa`, `MTN MoMo`, `Airtel`) settlements directly to the merchant's subaccount. |
| `stripe-custom` | Global Destination Charges (`US`, `CA`, `GB`, `EU`) | Routes funds via `transfer_data[destination]` and `application_fee_amount` directly into the merchant's connected Stripe account. |
| `dlocal` | Emerging Markets Split Settlement (`BR`, `MX`, `AR`, `NG`, `ZA`, `IN`, `ID`) | Executes local acquiring (`Pix`, `SPEI`, `UPI`, cards) with native merchant split instructions. |
| `tink` | European PSD2 Open Banking (`EUR`, `GBP`, `SEK`) | Initiates direct account-to-account (`PIS`) payments straight to the merchant's verified IBAN. |
| `bridge-split` | Regulated USD/EUR Virtual Accounts to `USDC` / `USDT` Liquidation | Converts incoming ACH/Wire/SEPA transfers into on-chain `USDC` or `USDT` delivered to the merchant's non-custodial wallet, minus `developer_fee_percent`. |
| `yellowcard-split` | Pan-African Fiat (`NGN`, `KES`, `GHS`, `ZAR`) to Stablecoin Ramp | Converts local African fiat collections into `USDC` / `USDT` settled directly to the merchant's self-custody wallet. |

For multi-currency transactions where the buyer's presentment currency differs from the merchant's settlement currency, see [Triple-Entry Ledger & Any-to-Any FX Splicing](/concepts/fx-splicing-ledger).
