## Roles

Invite people under **Settings → Team**. Each person has one role in a business:

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including the team. A business always keeps at least one owner |
| Admin | Everything, including the team |
| Developer | See everything, and manage API keys, webhooks and integrations |
| Finance | See everything |
| Analyst | See everything |

Someone in several businesses switches between them from the business menu at the top of the dashboard.

## Signing in

- **Passkeys** under **Settings → Security**: sign in with Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, a security key, or your Google or iCloud password manager.
- **Two-step verification** under **Settings → Security**: a passkey, an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, Authy and others; scan the QR code), or both. Recovery codes stand in for a lost phone. Text messages aren't offered: SIM swaps defeat them.
- **Sensitive actions** such as API keys, agent keys, payouts and settlement accounts ask you to confirm it's you, right where you are: use your passkey, an authenticator code or a recovery code. A confirmation lasts 15 minutes.
- **Signed-in devices** under **Settings → Security**: see where you're signed in and sign out other devices.

Dark mode is the **Dark mode** switch at the top of the dashboard; your choice is kept in that browser.
- **Single sign-on**: under **Settings → Business**, connect your identity provider with OIDC or SAML and prove you own your email domain. People with that domain then sign in through it.
