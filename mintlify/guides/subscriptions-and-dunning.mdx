Scaling recurring revenue globally requires deterministic proration math across time zones, idempotent usage metering down to the millisecond, and retry logic that adapts to regional payroll cycles.

## 1. Subscriptions & UTC Millisecond Proration

`createSubscription`, `calculateMillisecondProration`, and `changeSubscriptionPlan` manage recurring lifecycles (`Trialing`, `Active`, `PastDueRetrying`, `PastDueActionRequired`, `Paused`, `Canceled`) across `weekly`, `monthly`, `quarterly`, and `annual` intervals.

When a customer upgrades, downgrades, or changes seat quantities mid-cycle, PayRouta computes the exact unused credit and remaining period charge down to the **UTC millisecond**:

$$\text{unusedCreditMinor} = \text{roundHalfUp}\!\left(\frac{\text{oldPlanMinor} \times (\text{periodEndMs} - \text{changeAtMs})}{\text{periodEndMs} - \text{periodStartMs}}\right)$$

$$\text{newChargeMinor} = \text{roundHalfUp}\!\left(\frac{\text{newPlanMinor} \times (\text{periodEndMs} - \text{changeAtMs})}{\text{periodEndMs} - \text{periodStartMs}}\right)$$

```ts
import { createSubscription, changeSubscriptionPlan } from "@/lib/payments/subscriptions";

const sub = createSubscription({
  orgId: "org_1",
  customerId: "cust_42",
  customerCountry: "NG",
  plan: { planId: "starter", name: "Starter", currency: "USD", interval: "monthly", unitAmountMinor: "10000" },
  quantity: 2,
});

const { subscription, proration } = changeSubscriptionPlan(
  sub,
  { planId: "pro", name: "Pro", currency: "USD", interval: "monthly", unitAmountMinor: "30000" },
  3,
  Date.now(),
);
```

## 2. Timezone-Agnostic Millisecond Usage Metering

`UsageMeterStream` ingests usage events from edge workers and application servers worldwide:

- **Timezone Normalization**: Converts any ISO-8601 timestamp with regional offset (e.g. `+09:00` Tokyo, `+01:00` Lagos, `-03:00` São Paulo) into canonical UTC epoch milliseconds (`occurredAtEpochMs`) and records ingestion `clockDriftMs`.
- **Idempotency Deduplication**: Duplicate `idempotencyKey` deliveries within a subscription are dropped automatically.
- **Watermark Enforcement**: Rejects late events arriving after `windowEndMs + watermarkGraceMs`.
- **Aggregation & Pricing Models**: Supports `sum`, `max`, `unique`, and `last` aggregations priced via `flat-per-unit`, `package`, `tiered-graduated`, or `tiered-volume` brackets.

## 3. Global Smart Dunning & Regional Payday Syncing

Card and direct-debit declines happen for different economic reasons around the world. Retrying an `insufficient_funds` decline 24 hours later on the 11th of the month in a monthly-payroll market wastes retry quotas and triggers card-scheme penalties.

`scheduleSmartDunning` classifies decline codes and adapts retry timing to regional payday calendars:

| Decline Category | Example Codes | Smart Dunning Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Transient Network Error** | `network_timeout`, `issuer_unavailable`, `switch_timeout` | **Immediate Exponential Cascade** (`30s`, `5m`, `30m`, `2h`) with `cascadeToSecondaryAcquirer: true` |
| **Insufficient Funds / Balance** | `insufficient_funds`, `low_balance`, `exceeds_withdrawal_limit` | **Regional Payday Sync** — delays retry until the buyer's local salary credit window |
| **Expired / Replaced Card** | `expired_card`, `card_expired` | **Network Account Updater** — triggers Visa VAU / Mastercard ABU token refresh + 24h verification retry |
| **Soft Issuer Decline** | `do_not_honor`, `generic_decline` | **Cooling-Off Retry** (`24h`, `72h`, then synced to next regional payday) |
| **Hard Permanent Decline** | `stolen_card`, `fraud_suspected`, `invalid_account_number` | **Immediate Halt** (`shouldRetry: false`, transitions to `PastDueActionRequired`) |

### Regional Payday Calendars

| Region / Countries | Payroll Pattern | Smart Dunning Sync Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Africa & Europe** (`NG`, `GH`, `KE`, `ZA`, `DE`, `FR`, `NL`, `ID`) | `MONTHLY_25TH_TO_MONTH_END` | **25th of the month** at 09:00 local time (adjusted to preceding Friday if the 25th falls on a weekend), followed by the last business day of the month |
| **Japan & Sweden** (`JP`, `SE`) | `MONTHLY_25TH_STRICT` | **25th Gotobi / Löning salary day** (or preceding Friday) |
| **United Kingdom & India** (`GB`, `IN`) | `MONTHLY_LAST_WORKING_DAY` | **28th / last working day of the month** |
| **Americas** (`US`, `CA`, `BR`, `MX`) | `SEMI_MONTHLY_15TH_AND_MONTH_END` | Next upcoming **15th** or **last business day of the month** |
