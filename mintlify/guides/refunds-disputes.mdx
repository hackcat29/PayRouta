## Refunds

Refund a card, wallet or bank payment from its page in **Transactions**, in full or in part, as many times as what remains allows. Through the API, use `refund_payment` on the [MCP server](/integrate/mcp) with a key that has `refunds:write`.

- You fund every refund in full. PayRouta's fee on the payment isn't returned.
- Where the partner holds your balance, the refund comes out of it; where payments go straight to your bank, refund the customer from your bank instead.
- Crypto paid into your own NEAR Intents account is refunded from **Balances → Crypto**, where your wallet signs.

## Disputes

When a customer's bank disputes a payment, **Disputes** shows the amount, the reason the bank gave and the date you must respond by. The amount is held from your balance while the dispute is open.

Respond once, before the deadline, with what the customer bought, delivery details and any documents. A dispute you win releases the hold; one you lose sends it to the customer. You're notified of the outcome in **Disputes**.
