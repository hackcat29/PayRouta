Cards remain one of the most widely used payment methods globally. PayRouta routes card payments through merchant-owned acquiring accounts, ensuring full 3DS2 liability shift, direct merchant settlements, and intelligent multi-acquirer failover.

PayRouta never collects, transmits, or stores primary account numbers (PANs) or CVVs. All card credentials are collected inside secure provider iframes or tokenized directly with card networks.

---

## Supported Card Providers

| Provider | Method | Capabilities | Settlement Model |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [**Visa Direct**](/providers/cards/visa) | `card` | Global Visa debit/credit auth, real-time push to card | Direct scheme settlement |
| [**Mastercard**](/providers/cards/mastercard) | `card` | Mastercard network tokenization, Mastercard Send | Direct acquirer settlement |

---

## Same-Method Failover for Cards

PayRouta monitors provider error codes, API latency, and 3DS completion rates in real time. If your primary card provider returns an upstream timeout or 5xx outage:
1. PayRouta does not present an error to the shopper.
2. The payment is instantly re-attempted on your secondary card provider for that exact currency and region.
3. Cardholder authentication tokens (where supported) or 3DS challenge redirects are handled seamlessly without re-typing.
