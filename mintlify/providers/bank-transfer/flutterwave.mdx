**Method ID**: `bank-transfer`, `card`, `mobile-money`  
**Integration Mode**: `NATIVE`

Flutterwave offers pan-African payment infrastructure covering over 30 African currencies, barcoded merchant transfers, M-Pesa STK push, and global card collections.

---

## Integration Details

- **Adapter**: Native Flutterwave adapter with standard error mapping and idempotency verification.
- **Base URL**: `https://api.flutterwave.com/v3`
- **Supported Operations**: `pay-in`, `payout`, `settlement`
- **Webhook Signature**: `verif-hash` header validated against the merchant's configured secret hash.

---

## Capabilities

- **Bank Transfer (NGN, KES, GHS, UGX, ZAR)**: Real-time dynamic virtual accounts generated per checkout session.
- **M-Pesa Collections (KES)**: Automated STK push prompt to customer mobile numbers.
- **African Multi-Currency Payouts**: Automated merchant settlements across regional banking networks.
