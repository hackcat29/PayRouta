PayRouta provides non-custodial stablecoin processing. When a customer chooses to pay with stablecoins, they transfer tokens directly to an address that **you control**. PayRouta observes the blockchain network via RPC nodes and confirms the payment only after network finality depth is reached.

PayRouta **never takes custody** of tokens, never manages private keys, and never creates synthetic balances.

## Architecture & Payment Flow

1. **Payment Intent Creation**: The merchant creates an intent accepting method `stablecoin`.
2. **Deposit Address Allocation**: The merchant's configured wallet address (or an HD-derived sub-address verified during onboarding) is assigned to the intent with an exact token amount and precision.
3. **Payer Transfer**: The customer signs an ERC-20 transfer using their Web3 wallet (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Phantom) or direct exchange withdrawal.
4. **On-Chain Observation**: PayRouta's blockchain worker monitors mempool and block receipts using redundant RPC providers (Alchemy, Infura, local nodes).
5. **Finality Verification**: Payment transitions to `Confirmed` once the network's consensus depth is satisfied.
6. **Direct Settlement**: Funds are already in the merchant's wallet. PayRouta reconciles the transaction in the ledger without any fund sweep.

---

## Multi-Chain Failover

If gas fees spike or RPC latency on a particular network degrades, PayRouta can route the customer's checkout to an alternative supported chain (e.g. from Ethereum L1 to Base L2) while keeping the payment method within the stablecoins category.
