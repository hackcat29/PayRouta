---

## Features

- **Merchant-Owned Account**: Full access to the Coinbase Prime / Commerce dashboard.
- **Webhook Events**: Signed event streaming (`X-CC-Webhook-Signature`) for payment confirmation.
- **Assets**: Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDC, and Solana.
