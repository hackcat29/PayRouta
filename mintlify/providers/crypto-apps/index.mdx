Crypto app checkouts enable shoppers to pay with existing balances held in centralized cryptocurrency exchanges or custodial wallets without network gas fees or on-chain transaction delays.

PayRouta integrates with compliant institutional exchange checkout rails via merchant-owned corporate exchange accounts.

---

## Supported Crypto App Providers

| Provider | Users | Features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**Binance Pay**](/providers/crypto-apps/binance-pay) | 30M+ verified users | Instant off-chain QR code & app push, zero gas fees |
| [**Coinbase Commerce**](/providers/crypto-apps/coinbase-commerce) | Global | Hosted multi-chain crypto checkout, direct merchant custodial settlement |

---

## Architecture

Transactions execute off-chain within the provider's ecosystem. The merchant receives funds into their own verified merchant account with the provider, and PayRouta reconciles the transaction through standard status callbacks.
