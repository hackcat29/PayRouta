Revolut Pay enables fast, 1-click checkout for more than 45 million Revolut consumers across the UK, Europe, and international markets.

---

## Technical Specifications

- **Corridors**: United Kingdom (`GBP`), Eurozone (`EUR`), United States (`USD`), Switzerland (`CHF`), Poland (`PLN`), 25+ global currencies.
- **Checkout Experience**:
  - **Revolut Shopper**: 1-click biometric authorization inside the Revolut mobile app with face/fingerprint recognition.
  - **Non-Revolut Shoppers**: Fast guest card checkout with saved delivery details.
- **Settlement**: Rapid direct settlement into the merchant's Revolut Business multi-currency IBAN.

## Failover Strategy

PayRouta balances UK/EU wallet traffic between Revolut Pay, **Apple Pay**, **Google Pay**, and Faster Payments / SEPA Instant rails.
