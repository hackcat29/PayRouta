## Key Benefits

- **Highest Conversion Rate**: Eliminates manual card entry, yielding up to 50% higher checkout completion on mobile devices.
- **Biometric Security & 3DS Shift**: Leverages Secure Element hardware with dynamic cryptograms that qualify for fraud liability shifts.
- **Direct Merchant Processing**: Decryptable tokens can be processed by Stripe, Visa, or certified acquiring partners.
