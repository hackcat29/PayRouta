GCash is the Philippines' #1 financial super-app with over 80 million registered users, commanding more than 85% market penetration among Philippine mobile internet users.

---

## Technical Specifications

- **Corridor**: Philippines (`PHP`), cross-border merchant settlement in `USD` or `PHP`.
- **Payment Experience**:
  - **Mobile Web / In-App**: Seamless redirection to GCash app with MPIN or Face Unlock authorization.
  - **Web / Desktop**: QR Ph compliant dynamic QR code or SMS OTP web login.
- **Settlement**: BSP-regulated direct remittance to merchant Philippine peso bank accounts.

---

## Capabilities & Limits

- **Instant Auth**: Sub-2s settlement notification via verified webhook.
- **Limits**: Configurable based on the customer's GCash tier (Basic, Fully Verified, Plus).
- **Merchant Direct Relationship**: Direct contracting through Mynt merchant acquiring program.
