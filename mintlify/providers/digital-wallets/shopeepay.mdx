**Category**: [Digital Wallets](/providers/digital-wallets)  
**Provider**: Sea Limited (Shopee / SeaMoney)  
**Method ID**: `digital-wallet`  
**Integration Mode**: `STANDARD` / `CONFIGURABLE`

ShopeePay is SeaMoney's high-volume mobile digital wallet service, serving tens of millions of active shoppers across Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam) and Brazil.

---

## Technical Specifications

- **Corridors**: Singapore (`SGD`), Malaysia (`MYR`), Philippines (`PHP`), Indonesia (`IDR`), Thailand (`THB`), Vietnam (`VND`), Brazil (`BRL`).
- **Payment Experience**:
  - **Mobile Web**: Instant app-switch into Shopee app with biometric authorization.
  - **QR**: Scannable QR code compatible with regional national QR standards (QRIS in Indonesia, DuitNow in Malaysia, SGQR in Singapore).
- **Settlement**: Local currency bank deposit or SeaMoney corporate merchant balance.
