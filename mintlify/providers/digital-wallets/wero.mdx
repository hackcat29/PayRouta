**Category**: [Digital Wallets](/providers/digital-wallets)  
**Provider**: European Payments Initiative (EPI)  
**Method ID**: `digital-wallet`, `ideal`  
**Integration Mode**: `STANDARD`

Wero is the pan-European digital wallet initiative backed by 16 major European banks (including BNP Paribas, Deutsche Bank, ING, BPCE, Crédit Agricole) designed to provide a unified European alternative to international card networks, succeeding schemes such as iDEAL in the Netherlands and Paylib in France.

---

## Technical Specifications

- **Corridor**: Eurozone (`EUR`) across Germany, France, Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg.
- **Underlying Rail**: Built on pan-European SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst) with guaranteed sub-3-second interbank clearing.
- **Customer Experience**: QR code scanning or direct app redirection into the customer's native bank app or standalone Wero wallet.
- **Zero Chargeback Risk**: Irrevocable bank payment without card scheme fees or chargeback penalties.
