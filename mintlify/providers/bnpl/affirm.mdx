**Integration Mode**: `STANDARD`

Affirm delivers transparent, point-of-sale financing in the United States and Canada with zero late fees or hidden compounding penalties for consumers.

---

## Capabilities

- **Real-Time Underwriting**: Instant customer qualification based on purchase context and proprietary risk models.
- **Adaptive Checkout**: Dynamically shows monthly terms (3, 6, 12 months) and bi-weekly 0% APR split options based on cart value.
- **Enterprise Settlement**: Remitted directly to merchant commercial accounts via ACH.
