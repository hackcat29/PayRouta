Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) services allow consumers to split payments into interest-free installments or defer payment by 30 days while the merchant receives full settlement upfront.

PayRouta integrates with leading installment providers, absorbing zero credit or default risk on behalf of merchants.

---

## Supported BNPL Providers

| Provider | Regions | Plans Supported |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**Klarna**](/providers/bnpl/klarna) | Europe, United States, United Kingdom | Pay in 4, Pay in 30 days (Rechnung), Financing |
| [**Affirm**](/providers/bnpl/affirm) | United States, Canada | Real-time underwriting, 3–36 month installments |

---

## Merchant Protection

The BNPL provider assumes 100% of consumer credit and fraud default risk upon transaction authorization. The merchant receives gross settlement directly from the provider minus negotiated interchange fees.
